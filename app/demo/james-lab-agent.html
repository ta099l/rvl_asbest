What is built

In codeProject creation with the Project Information of the RVL template, field registration with a change log, evidence per lab report (file fingerprint, read method, linker version).
In codeReading lab PDFs with certificate and method columns, rule-based linking with typed codes (M, PBS, OS, ODS, HM), only unsure lines to the AI step, broken AI answers rejected.
In codeThe report in the RVL template layout: Part I lists, 2.3.1–2.3.7 with Source Records, survey limitations, Table 15/16 with quantities from the registration, sign-off by the Technical Responsible Person.
To confirmThresholds (lead 100 mg/kg as in the template), the Part I list and the asbestos code (M01 or MM01: two RVL documents disagree).
Stand-inThe AI step uses a stand-in. No real AI until the tool is approved.
App onlyRunning the linker on new samples, PDF upload, follow-up reports and the practice review: those run in the James app (python app/server.py), with fictitious data only.
